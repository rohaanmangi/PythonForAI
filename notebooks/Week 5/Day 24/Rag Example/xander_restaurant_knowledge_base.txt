Xander Restaurant was founded in 2018 by Ayaan Malik and Sara Ahmed in Karachi, Pakistan.

The idea began when the founders noticed that customers wanted a relaxed place where they could enjoy familiar comfort food, generous portions, and friendly service without a formal fine-dining experience.

The first location opened in Gulshan-e-Iqbal, Karachi. A few popular dishes and word-of-mouth recommendations helped the restaurant attract regular customers. Xander later expanded to other neighbourhoods while keeping its original focus on accessible food and welcoming service.
